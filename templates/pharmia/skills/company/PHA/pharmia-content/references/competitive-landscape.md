# Québec Pharmacist-AI Competitive Landscape (verified 2026-06-14)

Reference for **content** (positioning / "competitive alternatives") and **market-intel** (competitive digest).
Competitors to Pharmia (AI for QC pharmacists — Atlas research · Echo scribe · NoteGen · patient consultations).

## Direct competitors
- **Clinixio (clinixio.ca) — the main direct competitor.** Broadest QC feature-parity (AI intake + in-consult
  clinical support w/ sources + note/fax/Rx/lab-req gen + billing optimization), browser-native, no PMS
  integration. Claims ~200+ pharmacies. **Tech-founder-led** (Waseem Altarbouch / Nicolas Debray — NOT
  pharmacist-led). **Not yet TGV/SOC2-certified ("en cours").** Head-to-head fight → compete on product
  depth + QC-specificity + pharmacist-led trust.
- **Empego (empego.ca) — entrenched incumbent, REFRAME.** +750 pharmacies / +2.5M consultations; pharmacist-
  founder Philippe Chartrand; OPQ 2023 Innovation award; ~$300/mo; grant-funded. **RxPro integration**
  (Jean Coutu + Brunet rollout, newswire 2026-05-27). BUT it's **workflow-only** (consultations/suivis/
  logging): NO AI research, NO scribe, NO LLM decision-support. Its +750 base is **greenfield** for Atlas+Echo
  to layer on / displace — wedge = "you have the workflow, you're missing the brain," not "they're dying."
- **Plume IA (plumeia.ca) — AI scribe, HORIZONTAL not pharmacy-native.** Spans 10+ professions; pharmacists an
  afterthought in its priciest tier (~$150/mo). TGV-certified, physician-founders, Omnimed EMR, AQEPP partner.
  Threat is brand/cert, NOT product-fit — Echo's pharmacy-native note types (SOAP / Q1-Q2-Q3 / DAP + phone
  import) + the Atlas bundle out-tailor it.
- **IntelliSoins (Michael Ahern, B.Pharm)** — custom AI scribes AND ranks SEO for "scribes IA pharmaciens
  Québec" → product **and** content/SEO competitor to watch.

## General (NOT QC-tailored, low direct threat)
Heidi (global scribe that IS TGV-certified, aggressive free-Canada land-grab, still physician-focused);
Abridge / Suki / Nabla / Freed / DAX (none QC-tailored, none on TGV list). **QC-specificity (OPQ acts, RAMQ,
INESSS, Loi 41/31, P-10) is Pharmia's moat vs all of these** — lead with it.

## TGV (de-prioritized — do NOT pitch as a Pharmia play)
Pharmia/Echo are NOT on the MSSS TGV certified-vendor list; Plume IA + Heidi are. **CTO de-prioritized TGV
(2026-06-14)** — competitors may cite it, but do not recommend TGV certification as a competitive move.
